# Globetrottr

Globetrottr is a travel app that helps you discover places and record your trips.


## Sign In

- Create an account (if available), or
- Sign in with your existing credentials.

## Main Features

With Globetrottr you can:
- track your travel history
- view trip details
- compete with your friends
- discover land area
- complete quests, gain exp. and much more...
